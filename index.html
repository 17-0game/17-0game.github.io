How the 17-0 Game works

The 17-0 Game is an NFL roster-building and simulation challenge. You draft from random teams, eras and decades, so a 1960s lineman can share a huddle with a 2020s receiver.

  1. Draft nine positions

    Each pick shows three players from random teams and seasons. Take one and move on. There are no redos.

  2. Check your team rating

    Player ratings combine into one team rating. Quarterback and the lines count the most.

  3. Simulate 17 games

    Your roster faces 17 random team-seasons from NFL history. Win every game to finish 17-0.

Why a 17-0 season is so hard

In the Super Bowl era, only the 1972 Dolphins (14-0) and the 2007 Patriots (16-0) finished a regular season unbeaten. The Dolphins also won the Super Bowl, making them the only team to finish a full season and postseason without a loss.

The NFL moved to a 17-game schedule in 2021, which gives a perfect season one more chance to slip away. The 17-0 Game puts that challenge in your hands: even the best roster faces a real chance of an upset every week.

17-0 Game draft strategy

Use these tips to build a roster with the best odds of a perfect season.

Take the quarterback first

The quarterback has the biggest weight in your team rating. A 99-rated passer lifts the whole roster.

Do not neglect the lines

Offensive and defensive linemen count for a lot. Weak lines drag your rating down even with star skill players.

Avoid a single weak spot

A low-rated pick at any position costs you. Take a solid 94 rather than a risky pick from a thin group.

Play for the average

Your team rating is a weighted average. Consistent 95s beat one superstar and several holes.

Draft from every era of the NFL

17-0 Game FAQ

What is the 17-0 Game?

The 17-0 Game is a free NFL roster-building and simulation challenge. You draft players from random teams and eras, then simulate a 17-game regular season to see if your roster can go undefeated.

How do I win the 17-0 Game?

Win all 17 simulated games. Draft the highest-rated player you can at each position, and put quarterback and the lines first, since they carry the most weight in your team rating.

How many players do I draft?

You make nine picks, one for each position group: quarterback, running back, wide receiver, tight end, offensive line, defensive line, linebacker, cornerback and safety.

Is it possible to go 17-0?

Yes, but it is rare. Every game has a chance of an upset, even for an elite roster, so a perfect season needs strong picks and some luck.

Is the 17-0 Game free and official?

It is free to play in your browser. It is an unofficial fan-made game and is not affiliated with the NFL or any team or player.
