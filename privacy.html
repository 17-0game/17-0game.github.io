Privacy Policy

Plain-language details on how this site handles information.

Last updated: November 24, 2026

Summary

The 17-0 Game does not require an account and does not ask you for personal information to play. Your drafts and results are generated in your browser and are not sent to us.

Information we collect

We do not collect personal information through the game itself. If you contact us, we receive the details you choose to send, such as your name, email address and message, and we use them only to reply.

Hosting and server logs

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like most web hosts, GitHub may process technical data such as your IP address and browser details when you load a page. See GitHub's privacy statement for how it handles that data.

Third-party services

Pages load fonts from Google Fonts, which means your browser connects to Google's servers and your IP address is shared with them. If we add analytics or advertising in the future, we will update this policy and describe the cookies or identifiers involved before doing so.

Cookies

The site itself does not set cookies. Third-party services we use may do so under their own policies.

Children

The site is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Your choices

You can block third-party requests with your browser settings or extensions. To ask a question about this policy or request deletion of any message you sent us, use the contact page.

Changes

We may update this policy and will change the date above when we do.
