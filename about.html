About the 17-0 Game

A free NFL roster-building challenge for fans who think they can build a perfect team.

What is the 17-0 Game?

The 17-0 Game is a free browser game for NFL fans. You draft players from random teams, eras and decades, then simulate a 17-game regular season to see whether your lineup can go undefeated.

How it works

You make nine picks, one for each position group. Every pick offers three players. Your picks combine into a team rating, with quarterback and the lines weighted the most. Your team then plays 17 random team-seasons from NFL history.

About the ratings

Ratings are our own simple approximations of how dominant a player or team was in a given season. They are meant for fun and debate, not as official statistics. The season results are random, so even the best roster can lose.

Why we built it

Fans love arguing about the greatest players and teams of all time. The 17-0 Game turns that argument into a quick challenge you can play in a couple of minutes on any device.
